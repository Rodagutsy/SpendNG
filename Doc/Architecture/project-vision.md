# SpendNG — Project Vision

## Product Name
SpendNG

## Vision
To help Nigerian earners automatically capture, organize, search, and export their complete financial history across every bank and fintech platform they use without ever requesting a bank statement.

## Mission
SpendNG exists to eliminate financial blindness among Nigerian earners by turning transaction alerts that already exist in SMS and email into a structured, searchable, exportable financial record.

## Core Belief
Nigerians already generate financial data every day through transaction alerts. The problem is not missing data. The problem is fragmented data.

SpendNG solves this by passively collecting financial alerts and turning them into a unified financial timeline.

## What Success Looks Like
A user opens SpendNG and instantly knows:

- what came in
- what went out
- where money went
- how much remains
- what can be exported

without opening multiple banking apps.

## Product Principles

1. Passive capture over manual effort
2. Privacy before convenience
3. Speed over complexity
4. Trust through transparency
5. Exportable records are mandatory
