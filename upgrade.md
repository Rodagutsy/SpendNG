## **NIGERIA TAX GUIDE: What Your App Users Need to Know**

### **📌 Key Tax Authorities (2026 Reform)**

| Authority | Role | Taxes Collected |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS)** | Federal tax authority (replaced FIRS) | Companies Income Tax, VAT, WHT (corporate), Petroleum taxes, Digital economy taxation [^1_1][^1_2] |
| **State Internal Revenue Services (SIRS)** | State-level authority | Personal Income Tax (PIT), PAYE, WHT (individuals), Local business taxes [^1_1][^1_3] |

## **1. TAXCATEGORIES BY USER TYPE**

### **A. SALARY EARNERS (Employees)**

| Tax Category | What They Pay | Taxpayer Name | Rate | When/Due Date |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **PAYE (Pay As You Earn)** | Tax on salary, wages, bonuses, allowances | Employee (deducted by employer) | Graduated PIT rates (see table below) | Employer remits by **10th of following month** [^1_1][^1_4][^1_5] |
| **Pension Contribution** | 8% employee + 10% employer | Employee | 8% of monthly salary | Monthly [^1_1] |
| **National Housing Fund (NHF)** | Mandatory contribution | Employee | 2.5% of salary | Monthly [^1_1] |
| **Withholding Tax (WHT)** | On interest, dividends, rentals | Employee | 10% (dividends/interest) | Advance tax; creditable against PIT [^1_1][^1_2] |

**Taxable Income for Salary Earners:**

- Gross salary (before pension/NHF)
- Plus: any freelance income, rentals, side businesses, commissions
- **Taxable Income = Gross Income − Allowable Expenses − ₦800,000 tax-free threshold**[^1_6][^1_7][^1_8]

**Key Point:** If your employer has deducted PAYE, you **do not pay tax again** on that salary. But other income sources (freelancing, rentals) become taxable once total annual income exceeds ₦800,000.[^1_8]

### **B. STUDENTS (Including Student Influencers)**

| Tax Category | What They Pay | Taxpayer Name | Rate | When/Due Date |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Personal Income Tax (PIT)** | Only if earning > ₦800,000/year | Student (self-employed) | Progressive PIT bands | Annual filing by **March 31** [^1_6][^1_8][^1_9] |
| **Withholding Tax (WHT)** | On content creator payments, gigs | Student | 5% or 10% | Advance tax; creditable [^1_1][^1_2] |

**Critical Exemption:** Students earning **₦800,000 or less annually** (≈₦66,667/month) pay **0% income tax**.[^1_7][^1_9][^1_6][^1_8]

**Taxable Student Income Sources:**

- Social media influencer payments
- Freelance gigs (design, writing, coding)
- Tutoring income
- YouTube/YouTube monetization
- Commission from sales

***

### **C. FREELANCERS (Self-Employed Individuals)**

| Tax Category | What They Pay | Taxpayer Name | Rate | When/Due Date |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Personal Income Tax (PIT)** | Tax on **profit** (revenue − business expenses) | Freelancer (self-employed) | Progressive PIT bands | Annual filing by **March 31** [^1_6][^1_10][^1_11] |
| **Value Added Tax (VAT)** | On professional services (if turnover > ₦25M/year) | Freelancer | 7.5% | Monthly filing by **21st** [^1_12][^1_13][^1_14] |
| **Withholding Tax (WHT)** | Advance tax on client payments | Freelancer | 5% or 10% | Creditable against PIT [^1_1][^1_2] |
| **Direct Assessment Tax** | Self-employed tax assessment | Freelancer | PIT rates | Monthly/Annual [^1_15][^1_12] |

**What Freelancers Can Deduct (Reduce Taxable Income):**

- Internet/data subscriptions for work
- Software subscriptions \& apps
- Equipment (laptops, cameras)
- Office rent/co-working space
- Transportation to client meetings
- Marketing/advertising costs
- Bank charges \& payment platform fees
- Training/certifications[^1_10]

**What Freelancers CANNOT Deduct:**

- Personal food, family rent, vacations
- Personal phone bills (unless exclusive for business)
- Personal clothes[^1_10]

**Key Rule:** You pay tax on **profit**, not total revenue. Example: If you earn ₦200,000/month but spend ₦38,000 on business expenses, you pay tax on ₦162,000.[^1_10]

### **D. SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS**

Small business owners fall into **two categories**:

#### **Category 1: Business Name (Sole Proprietorship) Owners**

- **Legal status:** Owner = Business (not separate)
- **Tax:** Pay **Personal Income Tax** (not Companies Income Tax)
- **Same rules as freelancers** above[^1_1][^1_6]


#### **Category 2: Limited Liability Company (LLC) Owners**

- **Legal status:** Company is separate from owners
- **Tax:** Pay **Companies Income Tax (CIT)** + **PAYE** for salary

| Tax Category | What They Pay | Taxpayer Name | Rate | When/Due Date |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Companies Income Tax (CIT)** | Tax on company profit | Limited Company | 0% (if turnover ≤ ₦50M) or 30% (if > ₦50M) | Annual filing within **6 months** of year-end [^1_1][^1_2][^1_16] |
| **VAT** | On goods/services (if turnover > ₦25M) | Company | 7.5% | Monthly by **21st** [^1_12][^1_14] |
| **PAYE** | Tax on employee salaries | Company (for employees) | PIT rates | Monthly by **10th** [^1_1][^1_4] |
| **Tertiary Education Tax (TET)** | On company assessable profit | Company (except small) | 2% (old) → **3%** (Finance Act 2023) | Annual [^1_17][^1_18] |
| **Development Levy** | New levy replacing multiple legacy levies | Company (if turnover > ₦100M) | 4% of assessable profits | Annual [^1_7][^1_19] |
| **Capital Gains Tax (CGT)** | On sale of capital assets (land, shares) | Company | 30% (aligned with CIT) | On transaction [^1_20][^1_19] |

**Small Company Exemption (0% CIT):**

- Turnover ≤ **₦50 million** annually
- Total assets ≤ **₦250 million**
- Exempt from CIT, CGT, and 4% Development Levy[^1_20][^1_19][^1_16][^1_1]

## **2. PERSONAL INCOME TAX (PIT) RATES 2026**

### **Graduated Tax Bands (All Individuals: Salary Earners, Freelancers, Sole Proprietors)**

| Annual Taxable Income | Tax Rate |
| :-- | :-- |
| **First ₦800,000** | **0%** (tax-free) [^1_6][^1_7][^1_8] |
| **₦800,001 – ₦3,000,000** | **15%** [^1_6][^1_7][^1_2] |
| **₦3,000,001 – ₦12,000,000** | **18%** [^1_6][^1_2][^1_21] |
| **₦12,000,001 – ₦25,000,000** | **21%** [^1_6][^1_2] |
| **₦25,000,001 – ₦50,000,000** | **23%** [^1_6][^1_2] |
| **Above ₦50,000,000** | **25%** (top rate) [^1_6][^1_2][^1_21] |

**Top PIT Rate:** 25%[^1_2][^1_1]

**Taxable Income Formula:**

$$
\text{Taxable Income} = \text{Total Income} - \text{Allowable Business Expenses} - ₦800,000
$$ [^1_1][^1_6]

## **3. OTHER IMPORTANT TAXES**

### **Value Added Tax (VAT)**

| Aspect | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Rate** | **7.5%** (unchanged for 2026) [^1_7][^1_13] |
| **Who Charges** | Businesses with turnover > **₦25 million/year** [^1_1][^1_14] |
| **Filing** | Monthly by **21st** of following month [^1_1][^1_14] |
| **Zero-Rated (0% VAT)** | Basic food items, medical services, educational materials, books, exported services [^1_13][^1_14][^1_22] |
| **Exempt** | Oil/gas exports, baby products, shared-passenger road transport, land/buildings [^1_14] |

### **Withholding Tax (WHT)**

| Aspect | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Rates** | **5%** or **10%** (depending on service) [^1_1][^1_2] |
| **Purpose** | Advance tax collection; payer deducts and remits on your behalf [^1_1] |
| **Credit** | WHT receipts reduce total tax payable during annual filing [^1_1][^1_2] |
| **Dividends/Interest/Royalties** | 10% for both resident and non-resident [^1_2] |

### **Capital Gains Tax (CGT) - 2026 Changes**

| Aspect | Old Rule | New Rule (2026) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Companies** | 10% flat | **30%** (aligned with CIT) [^1_20][^1_19] |
| **Individuals** | 10% flat | **PIT rate** (progressive 15-25%) [^1_19][^1_2] |

**Key Change:** Government abolished the 10% CGT and consolidated it with income tax rates.[^1_20]

### **Stamp Duties**

| Aspect | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **What It Is** | Tax on legal instruments/documents (transfer of homes, buildings, copyrights, land, patents, securities) [^1_15] |
| **Individuals** | Pay on instruments executed by individuals |
| **Companies** | Pay on corporate transactions [^1_3] |
| **Exemption** | Salary transaction records are **stamp duty-free** (transfers < ₦10,000) [^1_23] |

### **Tertiary Education Tax (TET)**

| Aspect | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Rate** | **3%** of assessable profit (Finance Act 2023) [^1_17][^1_18] |
| **Who Pays** | Companies registered in Nigeria |
| **Exempt** | Small companies, sole proprietorships, non-resident companies [^1_18] |

### **Development Levy (NEW 2026)**

| Aspect | Detail |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Rate** | **4%** of assessable profits (before depreciation/losses) [^1_7][^1_19] |
| **Who Pays** | Companies with turnover > **₦100 million** |
| **Replaces** | Multiple legacy levies (TET, PTF, ICT TAX, etc.) [^1_19] |

```typescript
  const taxableIncome = Math.max(0, grossIncome - deductibleExpenses - TAX_FREE_THRESHOLD);
  
  const bands = [
    { min: 800001, max: 3000000, rate: 0.15 },
    { min: 3000001, max: 12000000, rate: 0.18 },
    { min: 12000001, max: 25000000, rate: 0.21 },
    { min: 25000001, max: 50000000, rate: 0.23 },
    { min: 50000001, max: Infinity, rate: 0.25 },
  ];
  
  let taxBeforeReliefs = 0;
  let remainingIncome = taxableIncome;
  
  for (const band of bands) {
    if (remainingIncome <= 0) break;
    const bandIncome = Math.min(remainingIncome, band.max - band.min + 1);
    taxBeforeReliefs += bandIncome * band.rate;
    remainingIncome -= bandIncome;
  }
  
  const finalTaxLiability = Math.max(0, taxBeforeReliefs - whtCredits);
  
  return {
    taxableIncome,
    taxBeforeReliefs,
    whtCredits,
    finalTaxLiability,
  };
}
```
